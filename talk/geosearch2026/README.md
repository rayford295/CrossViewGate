# GeoSearch '26 lightning talk

Presentation materials for *When Satellite and Street Views Disagree: Cross-View
Complementarity in Disaster Damage Assessment*, presented at the 5th ACM
SIGSPATIAL International Workshop on Searching and Mining Large Collections of
Geospatial Data (GeoSearch '26), Riverside, CA, 3 November 2026.

| File | Content |
| --- | --- |
| `CrossViewGate_GeoSearch2026_slides.pdf` | 17 slides, for viewing in the browser |
| `CrossViewGate_GeoSearch2026_slides.pptx` | editable source; speaker notes on every slide |
| `SCRIPT.md` | the full speaker script with cumulative timings, plus a five-minute cut |

All numbers on the slides are taken from the camera-ready paper
(`../../paper/geosearch2026_short/main.pdf`); figures are the ones in
`../../figures/`.
